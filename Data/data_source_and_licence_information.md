# Data Source and Licence Information

## Dataset
Daily historical precipitation data for Colombo and Hambantota, Sri Lanka.

## Source
[Open-Meteo Historical Weather API](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/historical-weather-api)

## Data Details
- **Variable:** Daily precipitation sum (mm)
- **Period:** 1 December 1990 to 30 September 2020
- **Locations:** Colombo and Hambantota, Sri Lanka
- **Time zone:** `Asia/Colombo`
- **Weather data model:** ERA5 reanalysis
- **Wet day definition used in the project:** Daily precipitation greater than or equal to 1 mm ($\ge 1\text{ mm}$)

## Purpose
The data is used to identify wet and dry days, estimate Markov transition probabilities, fit rainfall distributions, and simulate monsoon dry spells.
